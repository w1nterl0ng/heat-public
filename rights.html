RIGHTS & REMOVAL

REPORT A CONCERN

We want to address legitimate copyright, trademark, privacy, or other rights concerns promptly.

Do not post personal contact information publicly. The report form is public; provide your GitHub username if you need a maintainer to follow up privately.

What to include

  1. The specific page, track package, image, or file at issue.
  2. Your relationship to the material and authority to make the request.
  3. A description of the right you believe is affected and, where relevant, the original source URL.
  4. Your requested action: correction, attribution, removal, or another resolution.
  5. Your GitHub username for follow-up.

What happens next

We will acknowledge and review reports, may temporarily remove the material while reviewing it, and will update or remove content where appropriate. Good-faith creator credit errors can often be corrected quickly.

This site is an unofficial, non-commercial community sandbox. Names and marks belong to their respective owners. Nothing here claims endorsement by the owner or publisher of Heat: Pedal to the Metal.
