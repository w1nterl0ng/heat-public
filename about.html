ABOUT THE PROJECT

A PLACE TO EXPERIMENT

This is a fan-run, non-commercial sandbox for creating community track packages, testing strategies, and exploring digital play.

What it is

A beta project and track-creation playground. The Track Library hosts community-created packages after a manual quality and rights review. Creators receive credit on each track card.

What it is not

This project is not an official product, publisher partner, or substitute for commercial tabletop, Board Game Arena, or Steam offerings. It does not provide the official game’s rules. Support official releases and use official materials for rules and complete game content.

Community standards

We publish original or permission-cleared community content only. We do not intentionally host official board scans, copied board art, or material that infringes another party’s rights. See rights reporting or submit a track.

This is an unofficial, non-commercial community sandbox. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the owner or publisher of Heat: Pedal to the Metal.
